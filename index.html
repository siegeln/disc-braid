Disk Braid Simulator 盘编模拟器

Kumihimo · Kongo Gumi round braid. Colour the strands on the disk and preview the cord.

Tap a strand to paint it with the selected colour. Tap a dot's number to paint both strands of that pair.

Twist only changes how steeply stitches lean on the finished cord, not which colours show. If a real braid spirals more or less steeply than the preview, adjust it here.

Moves 编织动作

  1. Take the bottom-right strand up to the right of the top pair.
  2. Take the top-left strand down to the left of the bottom pair.
  3. Turn the disk so the next pair is at the top. Repeat.

One round means every pair has been to the top once. Strands at a dot only ever trade places with the dot opposite, so opposite dots decide each part of the pattern.
